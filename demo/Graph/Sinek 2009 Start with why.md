Sample watch notes for the demo; the times are rough.

[0:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4ZoJKF_VuA&t=30s) He opens with a puzzle: why do some people and companies lead when others with the same means do not?

[1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4ZoJKF_VuA&t=100s) The golden circle: why in the middle, then how, then what. Most organisations talk from the outside in, starting with what they make.

[4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4ZoJKF_VuA&t=250s) People don't buy what you do, they buy why you do it. He says it again and again, so it is clearly the claim of the talk. It reminds me of [[Busyness is a proxy for productivity]]: what we show is not the same as what drives us.

[6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4ZoJKF_VuA&t=380s) He ties the circle to the brain: the limbic brain decides, the neocortex explains afterwards. I am not sure the neuroscience holds up. Check it before I cite it.
