Zinsser treats writing as a craft rather than a gift. It is hard work for everyone, including professionals, and it improves with practice, not inspiration. You learn by writing regularly, by reading and imitating writers you admire, and by rewriting.

That makes the main obstacle getting started and keeping going. Clear's [[The two-minute rule makes starting easy]] fits well: commit to a tiny start and the rest often follows. Newport's [[Rituals reduce the willpower that deep work needs]] helps with the keeping going, since a fixed time and place removes a daily decision.

The craft view also lowers the stakes of a bad draft. [[Mistakes are part of learning]], and the fix is [[Rewriting is the essence of writing|rewriting]], not talent.
