Action is Arendt's highest category. Labor deals with the body and work with materials, but action goes on directly between people, without the mediation of things or matter. It is closely tied to speech: to act is usually to say something and to begin something in front of others.

Because it needs others, action depends on [[Plurality is the condition of action|plurality]], and because each person is new, it is rooted in [[Natality means every person can begin something new|natality]]. Action creates a web of relationships rather than a product, which is why [[Action is unpredictable and cannot be undone]].

I find it useful to ask where in my week this kind of activity happens at all. Real action is rare: a conversation that changes a decision, a public stand, a project started with others. Blixen telling stories to her guests in the evening ([[Fortællinger blev fortalt om aftenen]]) is a small, private example of speech between people.
