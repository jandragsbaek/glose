For Zinsser, bad writing is usually a symptom, not the disease. If you cannot write a clear sentence, the trouble is often that you do not yet know what you want to say. The two cannot be separated: clear thinking shows up as clear writing, and muddled thinking shows up as muddle.

So the first question is what you are trying to say, and he suggests asking it again and again while you write.

Ahrens pushes this one step further. Not only does clear thinking show in writing, but [[Writing is the medium of thinking]] in the first place. You often find out what you think by trying to put it down, and [[Writing gives feedback on what you have understood]].
