An implementation intention is a plan in the form: I will do this behavior, at this time, in this place. Clear cites research showing that people who write such a plan follow through more often than people who only have the motivation. The vague intention (I should exercise more) gives the brain no cue.

It works because it takes a decision out of the moment. When the time comes, you do not have to decide; you only have to follow the plan. In Kahneman's terms, it avoids asking a lazy System 2 to do work on the spot: [[System 2 is lazy and accepts what System 1 offers]].

Newport's rituals are implementation intentions for deep work: where, how long, under which rules. See [[Rituals reduce the willpower that deep work needs]].
