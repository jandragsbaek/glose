Productive meditation means using time when your body is busy but your mind is free (walking, running, showering, driving) to focus on one well-defined professional problem. When the mind wanders, bring it back. When it loops on the same facts, push to the next step.

Newport presents it as practice for concentration, like a workout for focus. It goes well with [[Embrace boredom to train concentration]].

I find it hard without anything to write on. Ahrens would say that thinking without writing is weak, because [[Writing is the medium of thinking]]. Maybe the best use of a walk is to prepare, then write it down at once. It also works because [[Attention is psychic energy]]: you choose where it goes instead of letting the phone choose.
