Newport suggests building a ritual for deep work: decide where you will work, for how long, how you will work (for example, no internet), and how you will support the work (coffee, a walk first). The fewer decisions you face when you start, the less willpower you spend.

He bases this on the idea that willpower is a limited resource. That idea, ego depletion, has not held up well in replications, see [[Ego depletion is a disputed account of self-control]]. I still think rituals work, but for the reason Clear gives: [[Environment design beats willpower]]. A ritual is a cue, and cues start habits.

The ritual is also a way to make starting easy, close to [[The two-minute rule makes starting easy]].
