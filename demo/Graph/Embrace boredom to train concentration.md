Newport argues that concentration is a skill, and that you lose it if every moment of boredom is filled with a phone. If you cannot wait in line without a screen, you will not be able to focus for hours when it matters.

His practical advice: do not take breaks from distraction, take breaks from focus. Schedule the times when you allow yourself the internet, and stay offline outside those blocks, even at home.

Csikszentmihalyi sees boredom as a signal: skill is higher than the challenge, see [[Flow lies between anxiety and boredom]]. Newport's view is different. Boredom is not something to escape, it is training. One way to use it is [[Productive meditation uses physical time for one problem]].
