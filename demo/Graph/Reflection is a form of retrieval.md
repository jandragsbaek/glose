Reflection means going back over an experience or a lesson and asking questions about it. What happened? What went well? What could I do better? What does this remind me of? Answering these questions makes me retrieve what I learned, which [[Retrieval practice strengthens memory|strengthens it]], and also connect it to other things I know.

So reflection is retrieval plus elaboration. Ahrens makes the same claim for writing: [[Elaboration turns information into understanding]], and I only understand an idea when I can relate it to others. Reflection also helps me notice the patterns behind single cases, which is how [[Mental models are built by extracting rules]].

In practice I can do this in a few minutes after a chapter or a meeting, in writing. A short note in my own words is already a reflection.
