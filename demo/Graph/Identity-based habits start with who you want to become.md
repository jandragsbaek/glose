Clear describes three layers of change: outcomes, processes and identity. Most people start with the outcome (lose weight). He suggests starting with identity (be the kind of person who does not miss a workout). Each action is then a small vote for that identity, and you do not need a unanimous vote, only a majority.

This is the deepest idea in the book for me, and also the one with the most open questions. Who chooses the identity? Kierkegaard would say that the problem is not choosing an identity but daring to be the self you already are: [[Fortvivlet ikke at ville være sig selv]]. Clear's version is much lighter, more practical.

Zinsser says something close about writing: [[The writer's voice comes from being yourself]]. You become a writer by writing like yourself, again and again.
