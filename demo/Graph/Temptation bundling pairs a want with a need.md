Temptation bundling means linking something you want to do with something you need to do. Only listen to your favorite podcast while at the gym. Clear bases this on research by Katy Milkman and on Premack's principle: a more likely behavior can reinforce a less likely one.

It targets the craving stage in [[The habit loop has four stages]]: the habit becomes attractive because the reward is attached to it.

I find it useful but a little sad. It assumes the needed task has no value in itself. Csikszentmihalyi points at the better case, where the activity becomes its own reward: [[The autotelic personality finds reward in the activity itself]]. Bundling may be a bridge until that happens.
