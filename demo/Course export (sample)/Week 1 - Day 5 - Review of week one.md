# Review of week one

> Sample course material for the Glose demo. Not a real course.

This week built one idea step by step: a model predicts text from patterns in its training, so anything it must know beyond that has to reach it through the prompt.

- Day 1: the model predicts the next token, and knows only what its training held.
- Day 2: a prompt is a program; give it structure and test it.
- Day 3: retrieval puts your documents into the prompt, and each step can fail apart.
- Day 4: embeddings find text by meaning, and miss exact detail.

Next week we build a small retrieval system end to end and measure each part of it.
